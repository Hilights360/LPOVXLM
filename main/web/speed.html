Clock choices follow hardware divider steps. The intermediate rate is approximately 26.67 MHz (80 MHz divided by 3).

Each run replaces playback and ends automatically, even if this page disconnects. It uses uniform brightness up to 10%, limited by regular brightness. Center fade and display duty are bypassed for this test; saved settings stay unchanged. No SD card or magnetic pulse is needed.

Watch a repeating 12-second cycle: all red, green, blue, white, RGBW blocks with a moving white marker, then alternating white and dark pixels. Each stage lasts two seconds. The block colors are offset between arms.
