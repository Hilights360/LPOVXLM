SD pin scope

Capture all six SD pins as one sample set, or select a single pin. The card stays inserted; playback stops and SD communication pauses. Pins are sampled one after another, not simultaneously. The card is remounted at its previous setting after the whole set.

What this capture measures

Samples are taken at the ESP32 pad, with SD transfers stopped. The selected pad changes to analog input and its internal pull-up is disabled; the board's external pull-ups remain. ADC sampling loads the line and adds its own measurement noise. This is not a direct measurement of the card's supply.

Timing comes from each conversion's actual timestamp. Wi-Fi and task scheduling cause gaps; unavailable readings appear as gaps, never as zero volts. The trace also breaks across long scheduling gaps. Voltage uses the chip's ADC calibration when available; otherwise the display uses raw ADC counts. The displayed range is the variation in recorded readings, not an isolated measurement of electrical noise. This board has pull-ups on CMD and DAT0-3 only. CLK has no pull-up and the ESP32 releases it during capture; its falling or low reading is consistent with an undriven line and is not evidence of a clock fault.

Captures stay in RAM until another SD tool is started or the controller restarts. In an all-pin set, every trace starts at its own time zero for comparison. The table and exports include each pin's start offset; matching positions across traces do not represent simultaneous events. Download a capture to retain its samples and settings. Playback remains stopped after capture.
