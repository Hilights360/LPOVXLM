Format SD card (erase files)

Formatting erases every file and partition on the SD card. This includes sequences, folders, firmware.bin, and the card's settings backup. Current controller settings stored in ESP32 flash are preserved.

Creates one FAT partition and mounts it again. The card must respond electrically; formatting cannot fix a bad connection. Keep power connected and leave the card inserted until the operation finishes.
